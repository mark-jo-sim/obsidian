## Background

Contact center workers do 2 kinds of work customers don't see:

- Synchronous (in-call) data logging
- Asynchronous (post-call), i.e. **after-call work** (ACW)

Agent assist $\Rightarrow$ *agentic* assist (AI agent performs actions rather than merely suggesting).
This is **workflow automation** (WFA).
