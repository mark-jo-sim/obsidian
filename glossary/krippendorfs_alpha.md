Ratio of **observed** to **expected** disagreement between annotators.

$$\alpha=1-\frac{D_o}{D_e}$$
More info at [UPenn white paper](https://www.asc.upenn.edu/sites/default/files/2021-03/Computing%20Krippendorff%27s%20Alpha-Reliability.pdf)
